Weather API Options

1. OpenWeatherMap (Best All-Rounder)

Pros: One Call API (detailed data), Generous Free Tier (1000 calls/day), Standardized.

Cons: Requires Credit Card for free tier, "Truly free" endpoint is less detailed.

2. WeatherAPI.com (Best for Ease of Use)

Pros: No Credit Card required, Accepts ZIP/City names, Clean JSON.

Cons: Free tier limited to 3-day forecast, Watermark required.

3. National Weather Service (NWS) (Best for US Users)

Pros: Completely Free (no key/card), High Reliability, Detailed 7-day forecast.

Cons: US Only, Occasional outages.

4. Tomorrow.io (Best for Hyper-Local Accuracy)

Pros: Hyper-Accuracy (street level), Niche data.

Cons: Complex Pricing, Response can be overwhelming.

5. Visual Crossing (Best for Data Analysis)

Pros: Single Timeline API, Budget Friendly.

Cons: Less focused on "pretty" icons, Learning curve.
